# Agropioo Landing Page — Design Rationale

> **What this is.** The thinking behind the landing page built in `apps/frontend`.
> It records the design decisions, the conflicts resolved, and the discipline applied so the page reads as **made, not generated**.
> Companion files: [brand-identity.md](./brand-identity.md) · [brand-colors.md](./brand-colors.md) · [landing-page-design.md](./landing-page-design.md).

---

## 1. The brief, restated

Agropioo is a Pakistan-first, AI-powered smart agriculture platform — a child product of **Aplinode** (http://aplinode.com). The landing page must feel **elegant, professional, exciting, and beautiful**, in **light mode**, strictly **within the brand's green palette** extracted from the Agropioo logo. Production-grade: Next.js + Tailwind v4 + TypeScript.

Three jobs for the page:

1. Say, in one glance, *what Agropioo is* — an AI agriculture advisor with farm memory.
2. Make it feel **grounded in soil, not stock-SaaS** — agriculture is about land and breathing room.
3. Drive one action: **early-access interest.**

---

## 2. Conflict resolved — two design systems existed

Two design-system files were present before the build:

| Source | Palette | Fonts | Origin |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|
| `docs/brand-identity.md` + `global.css` `@theme` | Logo-extracted greens (`#1C6428` canopy, `#013B1F` forest, `#3F8839` leaf, `#C1D8C1` sprout) + harvest gold `#D4A843` | **Playfair Display** (display) + **DM Sans** (body) | Derived from the Agropioo logo — the locked brand |
| `design-system/agropioo/MASTER.md` | Generic Tailwind greens (`#15803D`, `#22C55E`) + amber `#A16207` | **Space Grotesk** + DM Sans | Auto-generated by the ui-ux-pro-max tool on a generic "agriculture/farm tech" query |

**Decision: the brand-locked system wins.** The user explicitly asked to build *within brand colors*, the logo-derived palette is already wired into `global.css` as `--color-agro-*` tokens, and `landing-page-design.md` already commits to Playfair Display + DM Sans + the canopy/gold palette. The auto-generated `MASTER.md` used generic, off-brand greens and a tech-startup font (Space Grotesk) that fights the brand's editorial-elegant direction.

`MASTER.md` is **left untouched** (it records a prior decision and was not authorized for overwrite). Its component specs (button radius, input focus ring) are reasonable and compatible, but its **colors and fonts are not used** — `brand-identity.md` + `brand-colors.md` + `global.css` are the source of truth.

## 3. Macrostructure & genre

- **Macrostructure: Marquee Hero** — as named in `landing-page-design.md`. The hero is the page's signature; below the fold it becomes a feature rhythm, a journey, a statement, and a close.
- **Genre: editorial.** The brand calls for "organic editorial — clean white space, confident serif display type, rounded cards with subtle botanical curves, restrained green palette." That is the editorial voice: a humanist serif display carrying personality, a clean warm sans for body, hairline rules, generous whitespace.

This deliberately avoids the **SaaS-hero anti-pattern** (full-viewport centered single sentence + centered pill CTA) and the **3-column feature grid anti-pattern**. The hero is **left-biased**, not centered; the feature layout is **asymmetric**, not four equal icon-above-heading cards.

---

## 4. The signature element

> A large, soft-green hero panel combining the Agropioo logo with a flowing **furrow-curve contour motif** — the moment the page should be remembered by.

This is hand-built SVG: layered contour lines that read as **rolling farmland / furrows**, echoing the curved furrows at the bottom of the logo itself. It is semantic (it is *about* farmland), not decorative — which keeps it off the banned "aurora-blob" and "floating-orb" tells. The logo sits inside it. This is the one place boldness is spent; everything around it is quiet and disciplined.

---

## 5. Color use (summary; full detail in [brand-colors.md](./brand-colors.md))

- **Backgrounds:** `--agro-paper` white with soft mint `--agro-mint` / warm `--agro-stone` section fills — never flat pure white everywhere.
- **Primary actions:** canopy green `#1C6428`.
- **Primary conversion CTA:** harvest gold `#D4A843` with **dark forest text** (white-on-gold fails contrast ~2.3:1; forest-on-gold passes AAA). One gold moment — reserved for "Get early access."
- **Text:** ink `#0F172A` / slate `#475569` / forest `#013B1F`.
- **Borders / subtle fills:** sprout `#C1D8C1`.

Every color references a `--color-agro-*` token. No inline hex in components (locked-token discipline).

---

## 6. Typography

- **Display:** Playfair Display — elegant, editorial, agricultural-journal feeling. Used **sparingly**: hero headline, section anchors, the statement moment.
- **Body / UI:** DM Sans — modern, warm, highly legible on phones.
- **Headings are roman** (`font-style: normal`). No italic headers, no italicized emphasis words inside headings — a reliable AI tell, avoided.
- Type scale uses `clamp()` for the hero so it scales fluidly from mobile to desktop.

---

## 7. Copy principles

- **Lead with farmer benefit, not technology.** "Ask anything about your crop." beats "AI-powered agriculture platform."
- **No invented metrics, testimonials, or logos.** The features doc contains aspirational impact projections ("reduce crop loss by up to 30%"); these are **not** rendered as proven stats on the landing. A stat-led layout with no verified stats is the wrong layout — so there is no proof-bar with fabricated numbers.
- **Sentence case** for labels and buttons. Plain verbs: "Get early access," "See how it works."
- Voice from [brand-identity.md](./brand-identity.md): plain, practical, respectful, hopeful, local. Phrases reused: "Ask anything about your crop." · "Your farm history, always within reach." · "Built for Pakistan. Ready for the world."

---

## 8. Section-by-section reasoning

1. **Nav** — logo + wordmark left, two compact links, "Get early access" gold CTA right. Transparent over the hero, gains a subtle blur + hairline border on scroll. Avoids the static white-hairline "AI nav" by being contextual to the hero. Collapses to a sheet on mobile.
2. **Hero** — left-biased: small badge ("AI Agriculture Advisor"), Playfair headline "Ask anything about your crop.", a supporting line, two CTAs (gold primary + outlined secondary). Right: the furrow-curve + logo signature panel. Not full-viewport, not centered.
3. **Features** — four core capabilities from the project documentation (not the aspirational hackathon list): **AI Agriculture Advisor · Digital Farm Record · Local Languages · Weather-Aware Guidance.** Laid out asymmetrically — the Advisor leads larger — not as four equal icon-tile cards. Icons come from one hand-built SVG set (`icons.tsx`); no emoji.
4. **How it works** — the genuine farmer journey: Add farm → Ask the AI → Get guidance → Record activity. Numbering is **justified** here because the content is truly ordinal (a real sequence you do in order) — the one place section numbering is permitted rather than decorative.
5. **Pakistan-first** — the statement moment: "Built for Pakistan. Ready for the world." Lists the six supported languages. This is the manifesto beat — large Playfair type, mint or stone fill, restrained.
6. **Final CTA** — strong closing panel with an email-capture form ("Get early access"). Gold-on-forest or canopy background. One button.
7. **Footer** — a letter-close / statement footer: "A product of Aplinode," minimal links, copyright. Not a four-column link catalogue with a social-icon row.

## 9. Motion & accessibility

- **Motion-cut** (no animation library). One orchestrated fade-up on first load via CSS `@keyframes`; everything else is just there. `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses animation to opacity-only or none (already in `global.css`).
- **Mobile non-negotiables:** root `overflow-x: clip`; `minmax(0, 1fr)` on image/grid tracks; `white-space: nowrap` on buttons and nav links so no clickable text wraps to two lines; `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on display headers; sections collapse to one column at 320–414px.
- **Contrast** verified per [brand-colors.md](./brand-colors.md); body text ≥ 4.5:1, mostly AAA.
- **Focus rings** visible (`outline: 2px solid` canopy green), never animated in.
- **Touch targets** ≥ 44×44px.

## 11. What is intentionally NOT on the page

- No invented adoption numbers, "trusted by" logo walls, or testimonial quotes (no real ones exist yet).
- No fabricated impact stats rendered as proven facts.
- No pricing tiers (the product is pre-launch; "Contact sales" pricing on every tier is a tell).
- No fake browser/phone chrome around screenshots — the furrow motif + logo are the visual, hand-built in SVG.
- No emoji as icons; one consistent SVG icon set only.

These omissions are honest, not gaps. A landing that over-promises is a worse landing.
