# Agropioo Brand Identity

## Parent Company

**Aplinode** — [http://aplinode.com](http://aplinode.com)  
Agropioo is a child product of Aplinode, built to bring intelligent, accessible agriculture technology to farmers starting in Pakistan and expanding globally.

---

## Brand Essence

**Name:** Agropioo  
**Category:** Smart Agriculture / AgTech / AI Farming Advisor  
**Primary Market:** Pakistan  
**Future Vision:** Global adaptation for local crops, climates, languages, and practices.

Agropioo exists at the intersection of **soil and signal** — where farmland meets data. The brand communicates:

- **Growth:** Living crops, healthy land, prosperous farmers.
- **Intelligence:** AI recommendations grounded in local weather, history, and crop science.
- **Accessibility:** Guidance in the farmer's own language and context.
- **Trust:** Practical, timely advice that protects livelihoods.
- **Future:** Scalable technology that respects tradition.

---

## Logo

The Agropioo logo is a single organic droplet containing three ideas:

1. **A sprouting seedling** at the center — growth and agriculture.
2. **Circuit traces** on the left — AI, data, and technology.
3. **Curved furrows** at the bottom — farming, land, and harvest.

The droplet shape also evokes water, life, and sustenance.

### Logo Usage

- Use the logo on light or white backgrounds for maximum clarity.
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the seedling mark.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects such as drop shadows.
- The primary logo is full color. A monochrome dark-green version may be used when color is restricted.

- Text on `--agro-paper` should use `--agro-ink` or `--agro-canopy` for headings.

- `--agro-leaf` on `--agro-paper` is suitable for icons and large labels; avoid small body text.

## Typography

Agropioo's typography balances **human warmth** with **technical clarity**.

### Typefaces

| Role | Font | Usage |
|------|------|-------|
| Display / Headings | **DM Serif Display** or **Playfair Display** | Hero headlines, section titles, brand moments |
| Body / UI | **Inter** or **DM Sans** | Body copy, labels, buttons, navigation |
| Data / Utility | **IBM Plex Mono** or **JetBrains Mono** | Weather data, dates, farm records, metrics |

### Type Scale

| Token | Size | Line Height | Usage |
|-------|------|-------------|-------|
| `--text-hero` | 3.5rem (56px) | 1.05 | Hero headline (mobile: 2.25rem) |
| `--text-h1` | 2.5rem (40px) | 1.1 | Page titles |
| `--text-h2` | 2rem (32px) | 1.15 | Section headings |
| `--text-h3` | 1.5rem (24px) | 1.25 | Subsection headings |
| `--text-body` | 1rem (16px) | 1.6 | Body paragraphs |
| `--text-lead` | 1.125rem (18px) | 1.55 | Lead paragraphs |
| `--text-sm` | 0.875rem (14px) | 1.5 | Captions, metadata |
| `--text-xs` | 0.75rem (12px) | 1.4 | Badges, timestamps |

### Typographic Rules

- Headings are roman (not italic).
- Use the display typeface sparingly — one or two moments per page.
- Farm records and weather metrics should use the mono/data face to feel precise and trustworthy.
- Prefer sentence case over title case for labels and buttons.

## Voice & Tone

### Voice

- **Plain:** Explain farming advice the way a trusted local advisor would.
- **Practical:** Lead with what to do and when to do it.
- **Respectful:** Treat farmers as experts in their own land; technology serves them.
- **Hopeful:** Frame challenges as solvable and seasons as opportunities.
- **Local:** Welcome regional languages, crops, and customs.

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Onboarding | Warm, encouraging, simple |
| AI advice | Clear, specific, actionable |
| Alerts (weather, disease) | Urgent but calm, solution-first |
| Empty states | Inviting, guiding |
| Errors | Apologetic only when truly ours; otherwise explanatory and directional |

### Sample Phrases

- "Ask anything about your crop."
- "Irrigate in the cool hours before sunrise."
- "Your farm history, always within reach."
- "Built for Pakistan. Ready for the world."

## Imagery & Visual Style

### Photography

- Real farmers, real fields, real crops.
- Natural light; early morning and golden hour.
- Show hands working soil, water, plants, and tools.
- Avoid staged stock photography and generic smiling faces.

### Illustration

- Clean vector style inspired by the logo: simple organic shapes, rounded lines, limited palette.
- Use the circuit motif sparingly to suggest AI without feeling cold.
- Icons should feel hand-drawn, not hyper-corporate.

### Pattern & Texture

- Subtle furrow curves as a background texture.
- Leaf silhouettes or water-droplet motifs as accents.
- Warm paper texture for premium or heritage moments.

---

## Layout Principles

- Generous whitespace; agriculture is about land and breathing room.
- Clear vertical rhythm; information arrives in seasonal or lifecycle order.
- Mobile-first; many farmers will access the product on phones.
- High contrast for outdoor viewing conditions.
- Touch targets at least 44×44 dp.

---

## Brand in Motion

- Motion should feel natural: growth, unfolding, gentle transitions.
- Prefer easing curves that feel organic (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
- Avoid harsh bounces or overly mechanical motion.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## Application Checklist

When creating Agropioo UI or marketing materials:

- [ ] Use the primary green palette as the dominant color family.
- [ ] Pair a humanist serif display with a clean sans-serif body.
- [ ] Lead with farmer benefit, not technology features.
- [ ] Include local-language accessibility wherever possible.
- [ ] Test contrast for outdoor/mobile viewing.
- [ ] Keep the logo clear and uncropped.
