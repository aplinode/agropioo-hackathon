# Poultry — Broiler and Layer Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Poultry (broiler meat and layer eggs) is the largest livestock sub-sector in Pakistan. Two production systems: **broilers** (6–7 week meat cycle) and **layers** (18–72 week egg production). Success depends on biosecurity, vaccination, and environment control.

### Recommended Breeds / Strains
- **Broiler:** Cobb-500, Ross-308, Hubbard.
- **Layer:** Babcock, Hy-Line, Lohmann, ISA Brown.

## Housing
- Deep-litter or cage systems; dry, well-ventilated, draft-free houses.
- Brooders with 32–35 °C start, reduced 2–3 °C/week to 21–24 °C.
- Litter: rice husk / sawdust, kept dry and turned; remove wet patches.

## Feeding
### Broiler
- Starter (0–3 wk), grower (3–5 wk), finisher (5 wk+). Balanced crumble/pellets with 22–20% protein early, ~18% late. Clean water always.

### Layer
- Pre-lay (16–18 wk) and layer mash/pellet ~16–18% protein, 3–4% calcium for shell. Feed restriction avoids obesity before lay.

## Vaccination Schedule (typical)
- **Newcastle Disease (ND):** live + inactivated, repeated per schedule.
- **Infectious Bursal Disease (IBD / Gumboro):** maternal-antibody-timed live vaccine.
- **Infectious Bronchitis (IB),** **fowl pox** (wing-web), **avian influenza** (where programme exists).
- Follow a vet-designed schedule; record each dose.

## Diseases
### Newcastle Disease — MOST IMPORTANT
- Respiratory signs, nervous twitches, drop in production, high mortality. **Prevent with strict vaccination + biosecurity.** No treatment — cull and disinfect on outbreak; report to vet.

### Coccidiosis
- Bloody droppings, huddling, poor gain. Use anticoccidial in feed/program or vaccinated birds; keep litter dry.

### Infectious Bursal Disease
- Immune suppression in young birds; vaccine timed to maternal antibodies.

### Colibacillosis / Salmonellosis
- From poor hygiene; treat under vet direction; improve sanitation.

### Respiratory Complex (IB, mycoplasma, CRD)
- Snicking, sneezing; manage with ventilation + vet-directed medication.

## Pests / External Parasites
- **Red mite, lice** — treat housing and birds; clean between flocks.
- **Rodents** — secure feed, bait stations; they spread disease.

## Common Problems
### High Cumulative Mortality
- Usually broken vaccination, poor brooding heat, or wet litter. Audit schedule and environment.

### Poor Feed Conversion / Slow Gain
- Check feed quality, water, temperature, and disease.

### Egg Quality Issues
- Low calcium, heat stress, age. Adjust diet and cooling.

## Marketing / Harvest
- Broilers marketed at 1.8–2.2 kg; layers peak ~28–32 wk. Cull spent hens.

## Safety & Biosecurity Warnings
- **Restrict visitors, vehicles, and used equipment** — disease spreads fast.
- Disinfect footbaths, all-in/all-out flocks, and dead-bird disposal (bury/burn).
- Withhold meat/eggs per withdrawal period after any medicine.
- Any unusual high mortality: isolate and call a veterinarian immediately — notifiable diseases exist.
