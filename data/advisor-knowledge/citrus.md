# Citrus (Kinnow / Mandarin) — Fruit Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Citrus, especially **Kinnow** (a mandarin), is a leading export fruit of Pakistan, concentrated in Punjab (Sargodha, Mandi Bahauddin, Toba Tek Singh) and Sindh. Trees are evergreen; fruit matures Nov–Feb. Long orchard life; management centres on canker/greening control and fruit quality.

### Recommended Varieties
- **Kinnow** (dominant), **Feutrell's Early**, **Musambi (sweet orange)**, **Blood Red**, **Grapefruit**.

## Crop Calendar
### Flowering
- Blossom in spring (Mar–Apr); fruit set; harvest Nov–Feb by variety.

### Key Operations
- Pruning for light, weed control, and regular foliar nutrition improve yield and rind quality.

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Stages
- Fruit enlargement and summer heat need frequent water; stress causes small, puffy fruit and drop.
### Practical Schedule
- Young trees weekly; bearing trees every 7–12 days in summer, fortnightly in winter. Drip is ideal.

## Fertilizer Management
### Schedule (per mature tree)
- FYM / compost: 1–2 trolleys/tree/year.
- Nitrogen (Urea): 1.5–2.5 kg/tree split into 2–3 doses (spring, summer, post-harvest).
- Phosphorus + Potash: 0.5–1 kg each per tree.
- Zn, Fe, Mn foliar sprays correct common deficiencies (Zn is very common in calcareous soils).

## Diseases
### Citrus Canker (*Xanthomonas*)
- Brown raised spots on leaves/fruit with yellow halo; spreads in wind + rain. **Quarantine-significant.**
- **What to do:** use certified nursery stock; copper sprays per label in humid weather; prune and destroy infected shoots; avoid overhead irrigation that splashes.

### Citrus Greening / HLB (Huanglongbing)
- Yellow shoots, lopsided immature fruit, bitter juice. No cure — remove and destroy infected trees; control the Asian citrus psyllid vector with label insecticides; plant only clean budwood.

### Foot Rot / Gummosis (*Phytophthora*)
- Bark oozing at soil line. Improve drainage, bank soil away from trunk, copper paste on lesions.

### Scab
- Warty fruit/leaf lesions. Manage with copper at flushing.

## Pests
### Citrus Leaf Miner
- Silvery leaf trails on flush. Time pruning to one flush; spray dimethoate/spinosad per label only on new flush if severe.

### Scale, Mealybug, Spider Mite, Fruit Fly
- Manage with horticultural oil, label insecticides, and fruit-fly traps; conserve parasitoids.

## Common Problems
### Fruit Drop
- Physiological + water/heat. Steady irrigation and micronutrient sprays at fruit set.

### Puffiness / Peel Breakdown
- Excess N or water near maturity. Balance nutrition; reduce late water.

### Granulation (membranous pulp)
- In some varieties with age; select healthy budwood.

## Harvesting
- Harvest at full colour and sweetness; clip with stalk; handle gently; grade and cure; cold-chain for export.

## Local Measurements
- Mature tree yield 150–300 kg; orchard spacing ~5–6 m. 1 acre ≈ 8 kanal.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Observe PHI before harvest; copper can stain — follow label. Wear PPE; protect bees and water channels. Never move planting material from infected orchards.
