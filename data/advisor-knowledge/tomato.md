# Tomato — Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Tomato is grown year-round in Pakistan with spring (Feb–Mar) and autumn (Aug–Sep) being the main seasons. It is grown from transplants in beds with support. Highly perishable but very profitable.

### Recommended Varieties
- **Riogrande, Roma, Nagina, Sun-6021, NA-420, Monster** — determinate/indeterminate table and processing types.

## Crop Calendar
### Nursery
- Sow in protected nursery (trays or beds); transplant 4–5 week seedlings at 2–3 true leaves.

### Key Growth Stages
- Vegetative → flowering (3–4 weeks after transplant) → fruit set → fruit fill → maturity (60–75 days to first pick).

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Stages
- Flowering and fruit set are moisture-sensitive; stress causes blossom drop.
### Practical Schedule
- Drip or furrow every 4–7 days; avoid wetting foliage (reduces disease). Mulch to conserve moisture.

## Fertilizer Management
### Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
- FYM: 6–8 trolleys.
- Nitrogen (Urea): 2.5–3 bags split (side-dress after each pick).
- Phosphorus (DAP): 2 bags at planting.
- Potash (SOP): 1.5–2 bags — improves fruit set and quality.
- Deficiency of calcium/boron causes blossom-end rot — correct with foliar sprays if needed.

## Diseases
### Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV / TYLCV) — VECTOR = WHITEFLY
- Upward curling, stunting, yellowing; spread by whitefly. **Most important constraint.**
- **What to do:** control whitefly with yellow sticky traps + label-approved insecticides (e.g., Spiromesifen / Imidacloprid) early; use virus-free nursery; avoid sowing near infected fields; remove infected plants.

### Early Blight (*Alternaria*) / Late Blight (*Phytophthora*)
- Leaf spots and blight in humid weather. Spray Mancozeb / Chlorothalonil per label; ensure airflow.

### Bacterial Wilt
- Sudden wilt, brown vessels. Use resistant rootstocks / healthy nursery; improve drainage.

### Fruit Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- Caterpillars bore into fruits. Spray Helicoverpa-specific Bt (*Bacillus thuringiensis*) or label insecticides at egg/early larval stage; scout regularly.

## Pests
### Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*)
- Also vectors leaf curl. Manage as above.

### Aphids, Leaf Miner, Spider Mite
- Monitor; use neem oil, predatory mites, or label insecticides when thresholds exceed.

## Common Problems
### Blossom Drop
- Heat or water stress at flowering. Irrigate and provide shade/ventilation in nurseries.

### Blossom-End Rot
- Calcium deficiency / uneven water. Steady moisture + foliar calcium.

### Cracking / Catfacing
- Irregular water; keep even irrigation near maturity.

## Harvesting
- Pick firm, full-colour fruits every 3–5 days; handle gently; cool quickly. Market within 1–2 days or cold-store.

## Local Measurements
- Plant population ≈ 8,000–10,000/acre. Yield 200–400 maund/acre for healthy crops. 1 acre ≈ 8 kanal.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Observe PHI strictly on fruiting crops — never spray close to harvest. Wear PPE; avoid spraying in bloom to protect pollinators. Rinse and dispose containers safely.
