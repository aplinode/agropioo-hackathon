# Mung Bean (Moong) — Complete Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Mung bean is an important pulse crop in Pakistan, grown on approximately 0.3 million hectares. It is cultivated in both Kharif (summer) and Rabi (winter) seasons. Pakistan produces about 0.2 million tons annually. Mung is valued for its protein content (24%) and ability to fix atmospheric nitrogen.

## Crop Calendar

### Sowing
- **Kharif sowing:** May–June (after wheat harvest)
- **Rabi sowing:** September–October (early sowing preferred)
- **Seed rate:** 20–25 kg per hectare (approximately 8–10 kg per acre)
- **Seed treatment:** Thiram at 2.5g per kg seed
- **Spacing:** Row-to-row 30 cm, plant-to-plant 10 cm

### Key Growth Stages
| Stage | Days After Sowing | Critical Management |
|---|---|---|
| Germination | 5–7 | Uniform moisture |
| Vegetative | 10–30 | Weed management |
| Flowering | 30–45 | Critical water need |
| Pod filling | 45–60 | Avoid water stress |
| Maturity | 60–75 | Harvest timing critical |

### Irrigation Schedule
1. **First irrigation:** 15–20 days after sowing
2. **Second irrigation:** 35–40 days (flowering)
3. **Third irrigation:** 50–55 days (pod filling) — if needed

Mung is relatively drought-tolerant but yields significantly more with 2–3 irrigations.

## Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
| Nutrient | Basal (at sowing) | Top-dress |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 5 kg (11 kg Urea) | — (fixes own N) |
| Phosphorus | 20 kg (44 kg DAP) | — |
| Potassium | 10 kg (17 kg SOP) | — |

Total NPK: 5-20-10 kg per acre. Inoculate seed with Rhizobium for better nitrogen fixation.

## Major Diseases

### Yellow Mosaic Virus
- **Symptoms:** Bright yellow patches on leaves, stunted growth
- **Vector:** Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- **Management:** Control whitefly with Imidacloprid, remove infected plants

### Cercospora Leaf Spot
- **Symptoms:** Brown spots with gray centers on leaves
- **Management:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5g/L water

### Root Rot
- **Symptoms:** Wilting, brown roots, plant death
- **Favorable conditions:** Waterlogged soil
- **Management:** Ensure proper drainage, treat seed with Thiram

## Major Pests

### Pod Borer (Helicoverpa)
- **Symptoms:** Larvae feed on developing pods
- **Management:** Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG at 0.4g/L at flowering

### Aphids
- **Symptoms:** Sucking sap, leaf curling, sticky honeydew
- **Management:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/L

### Whitefly
- **Symptoms:** Sucking sap, transmits yellow mosaic virus
- **Management:** Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP at 1.2g/L

## Harvesting and Storage
- Harvest when 80% of pods turn brown/black
- Cut plants at base, stack for 3–5 days to dry
- Thresh by beating or mechanical threshing
- Clean and store in moisture-proof bags
- Expected yield: 8–12 maunds per acre (320–480 kg/acre)

## Economic Considerations
- Seed cost: Rs 150–250 per kg
- Input cost per acre: Rs 15,000–20,000
- Expected revenue: Rs 40,000–60,000 per acre at Rs 10,000–12,000/maund
- Important rotation crop — fixes nitrogen, improves soil health
