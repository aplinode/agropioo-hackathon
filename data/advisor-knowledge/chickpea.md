# Chickpea (Gram) — Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Chickpea (gram) is the most important Rabi pulse in Pakistan, grown on residual moisture after rice or cotton in Punjab and Sindh, and under rainfed conditions in Potohar and Balochistan. It fixes atmospheric nitrogen, improving soil for the following crop.

### Recommended Varieties
- **Punjab-2008, Noor-2009, Bittle-98, Karakoram-99, Bhakkar-2001** — disease-tolerant, high-yield types.

## Crop Calendar
### Sowing
- Sow by mid-November in irrigated areas; rainfed by end of October. Late sowing cuts yield sharply.
- Row spacing 30–45 cm, plant to a depth of 5–7 cm.

### Key Growth Stages
- Germination → vegetative → flowering (Feb–Mar) → pod filling → maturity (April–May, 120–140 days).

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Stages
- One pre-flowering (boot) irrigation is the most yield-saving if rain is short; a second at pod fill helps on light soils.
### Practical Schedule
- Mostly rainfed. One irrigation at flowering if dry; avoid excess water (causes vegetative growth, less pod set).

## Fertilizer Management
### Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
- Usually NO nitrogen needed (fixes its own); add only if very poor soil: 1 bag Urea at sowing.
- Phosphorus (DAP): 1–1.5 bags at sowing.
- Rhizobium + PSB seed treatment improves nodulation and phosphorus uptake.

## Diseases
### Ascochyta Blight (*Ascochyta rabiei*) — MOST DAMAGING
- Pale brown leaf spots with dark margins, stem cankers, pod spotting; spreads in cool wet weather.
- **What to do:** use certified, treated seed; spray Mancozeb / Chlorothalonil per label at first spots; avoid dense sowing for airflow.

### Fusarium Wilt
- Yellowing, wilting, brown vessels. Use resistant varieties and crop rotation (avoid gram after gram).

### Root Rot (Dry Root Rot)
- In hot dry springs; reduce by timely sowing and balanced phosphorus.

## Pests
### Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- Caterpillars on flowers/pods. Spray Bt or label insecticide at flowering; scout.

### Aphids, Whitefly, Termites
- Manage with label insecticides only when above threshold; protect bees during flowering.

## Common Problems
### Poor Nodulation
- Seed not inoculated. Treat seed with Rhizobium + PSB before sowing.

### Excessive Vegetative Growth
- Too much N or irrigation. Avoid extra nitrogen.

### Empty / Few Pods
- Heat or moisture stress at flowering. Timely sowing avoids the hot, dry flowering window.

## Harvesting
- Harvest when pods dry and leaves shed (April–May). Cut, dry, thresh; store at <10% moisture to avoid aflatoxin.

## Local Measurements
- Seed rate ≈ 1.5–2 maund/acre (treated). Yield 8–20 maund/acre (irrigated 20–30). 1 acre ≈ 8 kanal.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Spray at evening to protect bees; wear PPE; observe PHI. Store grain dry to prevent mould and toxin formation.
