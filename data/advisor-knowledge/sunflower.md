# Sunflower — Complete Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Sunflower is an important oilseed crop in Pakistan, grown on approximately 0.3 million hectares. It is cultivated in both Kharif (summer) and Rabi (winter) seasons. Pakistan produces about 0.6 million tons of sunflower seed annually. Major varieties include S-278, S-6162, S-6171, and Hybrid-6620.

## Crop Calendar

### Sowing
- **Kharif sowing:** May–June (after wheat harvest)
- **Rabi sowing:** September–October (early sowing preferred)
- **Seed rate:** 8–10 kg per hectare (approximately 3–4 kg per acre)
- **Seed treatment:** Thiram at 2.5g per kg seed before sowing
- **Spacing:** Row-to-row 60 cm, plant-to-plant 30 cm

### Key Growth Stages
| Stage | Days After Sowing | Critical Management |
|---|---|---|
| Germination | 7–10 | Ensure adequate soil moisture |
| Vegetative | 15–40 | Weed management, first irrigation |
| Bud formation | 40–55 | Second irrigation, phosphorus application |
| Flowering | 55–75 | Critical water need, avoid stress |
| Seed filling | 75–100 | Third irrigation, monitor for diseases |
| Maturity | 100–120 | Harvest when back of head turns yellow |

### Irrigation Schedule
1. **First irrigation:** 20–25 days after sowing (vegetative stage)
2. **Second irrigation:** 40–45 days (bud formation)
3. **Third irrigation:** 60–65 days (flowering)
4. **Fourth irrigation:** 80–85 days (seed filling) — if needed

Apply 50–60 mm per irrigation. Sunflower is drought-tolerant but yields significantly with timely irrigation.

## Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
| Nutrient | Basal (at sowing) | Top-dress |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 20 kg (45 kg Urea) | 20 kg at bud stage |
| Phosphorus | 30 kg (65 kg DAP) | — |
| Potassium | 15 kg (25 kg SOP) | — |

Total NPK: 40-30-15 kg per acre. Apply zinc sulphate 10 kg/acre if soil is zinc-deficient.

## Major Diseases

### Alternaria Leaf Spot
- **Symptoms:** Brown to dark spots with concentric rings on leaves
- **Favorable conditions:** Warm (25–30°C), humid weather
- **Management:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5g/L water at first appearance

### Rust
- **Symptoms:** Small brown pustules on leaves, stems, and bracts
- **Favorable conditions:** Cool, humid weather
- **Management:** Spray Propiconazole 25% EC at 1ml/L water

### Head Rot
- **Symptoms:** Rotting of the flower head, white fungal growth
- **Favorable conditions:** High humidity during flowering
- **Management:** Ensure good drainage, avoid overhead irrigation

## Major Pests

### Sunflower Moth
- **Symptoms:** Larvae bore into flower head, feed on developing seeds
- **Management:** Spray Chlorpyrifos 40% EC at 2ml/L when moths are seen

### Aphids
- **Symptoms:** Sucking sap from leaves, sticky honeydew secretion
- **Management:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/L water

### Caterpillars (Semilooper, Defoliator)
- **Symptoms:** Defoliation, feeding on leaves and stems
- **Management:** Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG at 0.4g/L water

## Harvesting and Storage
- Harvest when back of flower head turns yellow-brown
- Cut heads and dry in sun for 5–7 days
- Thresh by beating heads with sticks
- Store seeds in moisture-proof bags at <8% moisture
- Expected yield: 15–20 maunds per acre (600–800 kg/acre)

## Economic Considerations
- Seed cost: Rs 800–1,200 per kg (hybrid) or Rs 400–600 (open-pollinated)
- Input cost per acre: Rs 25,000–35,000 (including seed, fertilizer, pesticide, labor)
- Expected revenue: Rs 40,000–60,000 per acre at Rs 5,000–6,000/maund
- Oil extraction rate: 38–42%
