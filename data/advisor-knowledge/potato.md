# Potato — Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Potato is a major Rabi vegetable grown across Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan (especially the Quetta/Patfeeder belt). Two main seasons: autumn (planted Sep–Oct, harvested Dec–Feb) and spring (planted Jan–Feb, harvested Apr–Jun). It is a high-value, short-duration crop grown from certified seed tubers (not true seed).

### Recommended Varieties
- **Cardinal, Ultimus, SH-5, Desiree** — table and processing.
- **Lady Rosetta** — crisping/chips.
- **Ranger, Faisal** — early maturity.

## Crop Calendar
### Sowing Windows
- Autumn crop: mid-September to mid-October.
- Spring crop: mid-January to mid-February.

### Key Growth Stages
- Sprout emergence (15–25 days) → vegetative growth → tuber initiation (after 30–40 days) → tuber bulking → maturity (90–120 days depending on variety).

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Irrigation Stages
- Tuber initiation and bulking are the most water-sensitive stages.
### Practical Schedule
- First irrigation 20–25 days after planting, then every 8–10 days.
- Stop irrigation 10–15 days before harvest to firm up skins and avoid tuber rot.

## Fertilizer Management
### Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
- FYM / compost: 8–10 trolleys at land preparation.
- Nitrogen (Urea): 3–4 bags (split — half at planting, rest at earthing-up).
- Phosphorus (DAP): 2 bags at planting.
- Potash (SOP): 1–1.5 bags at planting.
- On sandy soils add a zinc sulphate top dressing if deficiency shows.

## Diseases
### Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*) — MOST DESTRUCTIVE
Water-soaked dark lesions on leaves, white fungal growth underneath, rapid blight in cool humid weather.
- **What to do:** spray Mancozeb 80% WP @ 2.5–3 kg/acre (or Dimethomorph + Mancozeb) at first sign; repeat every 7–10 days in wet weather. **Follow label dose and PHI.**
- **Caution:** do not enter field when foliage is wet; protect from drift onto other crops.

### Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)
- Brown concentric-ring spots on older leaves; reduces yield.

### Black Scurf (*Rhizoctonia solani*)
- Dark scurf on tubers; poor emergence. Use healthy certified seed and treat with Thiram/Captan before planting.

### Bacterial Wilt
- Plants wilt suddenly; vascular browning. Use resistant varieties and avoid infected seed plots.

## Pests
### Potato Tuber Moth (*Phthorimaea operculella*)
- Larvae tunnel tubers in storage. Cure and store in cool, dark, ventilated stores; dust with permissible protectants per label.

### Aphids and Whitefly
- Spread viral diseases; manage with neem oil or label-approved insecticides. Protect natural enemies.

### Cutworm / Looper
- Chew seedlings. Treat seed furrow or seedlings if damage is high.

## Common Problems
### Small / Few Tubers
- Caused by poor seed size, low potash, or drought at bulking. Use 40–50 g seed tubers, keep soil moist at bulking.

### Green Tubers
- Exposure to light. Hill up soil (earthing-up) to cover tubers.

### Hollow Heart
- Irregular watering / rapid growth. Keep even moisture.

## Harvesting
- Harvest when vines dry and skins are set (90–120 days). Dig carefully to avoid bruising.
- Cure 2–3 hours in shade, then store at 4–10 °C in dark, ventilated stores.

## Local Measurements
- 1 acre ≈ 8 kanal. Seed rate ≈ 8–10 maund/acre (≈ 20–25 trolleys of tubers). Yield 150–300 maund/acre for healthy crops.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Wear gloves, mask, and goggles when spraying. Observe Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) printed on the label — never spray close to harvest. Wash hands and equipment after use. Keep chemicals away from food, animals, and water channels.
