# Practical Guide: Pesticide Spraying in Pakistan

## Overview
This guide provides practical instructions for safe and effective pesticide application. Always read the product label before use. Follow the recommended dosage — more is NOT better and can harm crops, beneficial insects, and your health.

## Safety First — Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

### Mandatory PPE
- **Clothing:** Full-sleeve shirt, long pants, closed-toe shoes
- **Hands:** Chemical-resistant gloves (nitrile or rubber)
- **Face:** Mask or respirator (N95 minimum, half-face respirator for concentrates)
- **Eyes:** Safety goggles (especially for liquid concentrates)
- **Head:** Cap or hat

### During Spraying
- Do not eat, drink, or smoke while spraying
- Stay upwind of the spray drift
- Avoid spraying in strong winds (>15 km/h)
- Take breaks every 30–45 minutes
- If you feel dizzy or nauseous, stop immediately, move to fresh air, and wash

### After Spraying
- Wash all PPE thoroughly with soap and water
- Bathe and change clothes before eating or sleeping
- Wash spray equipment thoroughly
- Store pesticides in original containers, locked away from children and livestock

## When to Spray — Timing Matters

### Best Time of Day
- **Early morning (6–9 AM):** Best for most sprays — low wind, moderate temperature, dew helps absorption
- **Late afternoon (4–6 PM):** Second best — cooler temperatures, less evaporation
- **Avoid midday (11 AM–3 PM):** High temperature causes rapid evaporation and reduced effectiveness

### Weather Conditions
| Condition | Spray? | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Wind < 10 km/h | YES | Ideal conditions |
| Wind 10–15 km/h | CAUTION | Spray drift risk, use larger droplets |
| Wind > 15 km/h | NO | Spray will drift to neighboring crops |
| Temperature > 35°C | AVOID | Chemical evaporates too fast |
| Temperature < 15°C | AVOID | Insects are less active, reduced uptake |
| Rain expected within 4 hours | NO | Washoff before absorption |
| Humidity > 80% | YES | Good for systemic pesticides |

### Crop Stage Considerations
- **Avoid spraying during flowering** when possible — kills pollinators
- **Avoid spraying close to harvest** — follow pre-harvest interval (PHI) on label
- **Spray in morning when bees are inactive** — bees start foraging at ~9 AM

## How to Mix Pesticides

### Step-by-Step
1. Fill spray tank halfway with clean water
2. Add the required amount of pesticide concentrate
3. Mix thoroughly for 2–3 minutes
4. Fill the rest of the tank with water
5. Mix again for 1–2 minutes
6. Calibrate sprayer before use

### Important Rules
- **Never mix different pesticides** unless label specifically allows it
- **Never use food containers** for mixing or storing pesticides
- **Mix only the amount needed** — don't store leftover mixed spray
- **Use clean water** — dirty or saline water can reduce effectiveness

## Spray Equipment Calibration

### Knapsack Sprayer (Most Common)
- **Capacity:** 15–20 liters
- **Nozzle:** Flat fan, 1.0–1.5 mm orifice
- **Pressure:** 20–30 PSI (pump 30–40 strokes)
- **Speed:** Walk at steady pace, ~4 km/h
- **Coverage:** 15–20 liters covers 1 acre (depending on crop density)

### Calibration Check
1. Fill tank with plain water
2. Spray a measured area (e.g., 1/10 acre)
3. Measure water used
4. Calculate: Tank capacity ÷ water used per 1/10 acre = total area per tank

## Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)
| Crop | Minimum Days Before Harvest |
|---|---|
| Wheat | 15 days |
| Cotton | 10 days |
| Rice | 15 days |
| Vegetables | 7–14 days (varies by product) |
| Fruits | 7–21 days (varies by product) |

**Always check the product label for the specific PHI**

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM)
1. **Scout first:** Check crop regularly — don't spray on schedule, spray when needed
2. **Identify correctly:** Know the pest before choosing the pesticide
3. **Use biological control:** Encourage natural predators (ladybugs, parasitic wasps)
4. **Rotate chemicals:** Alternate between different chemical groups to prevent resistance
5. **Use threshold levels:** Not every pest requires spraying — economic threshold levels exist
6. **Cultural practices:** Crop rotation, resistant varieties, proper spacing reduce pest pressure

## Emergency — If Poisoned
- **Skin contact:** Remove contaminated clothing, wash with soap and water for 15 minutes
- **Eye contact:** Rinse with clean water for 15 minutes, seek medical help
- **Inhaled:** Move to fresh air, keep warm, seek medical help if symptoms persist
- **Swallowed:** Do NOT induce vomiting, sip water, seek immediate medical help
- **Bring the pesticide container** to the doctor/hospital
