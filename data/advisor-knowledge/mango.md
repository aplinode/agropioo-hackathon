# Mango — Fruit Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Mango is the national fruit and a major summer crop. Leading varieties: Sindhri, Chaunsa, Dussehri, Langra, Anwar Ratol, Fajri. Grown in Sindh, Punjab, and parts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Orchards are long-lived; management focuses on flowering, fruit set, and post-harvest.

### Recommended Varieties
- **Sindhri** (early, Sindh), **Chaunsa** (premium, Punjab), **Dussehri/Langra** (north), **Anwar Ratol** (small, aromatic).

## Crop Calendar
### Flowering
- Panicles appear Dec–Feb; fruit set Feb–Mar; harvest May–Sep by variety (Sindhri earliest, Chaunsa mid, Ratol late).

### Key Operations
- Bloom management, fruit thinning, and bagging improve grade.

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Stages
- Flowering to fruit set and fruit enlargement need steady water; water stress causes fruit drop.
### Practical Schedule
- Young trees: weekly. Bearing trees: every 10–15 days; reduce near maturity to improve flavour and reduce splitting.

## Fertilizer Management
### Schedule (per mature tree)
- FYM / compost: 1–2 trolleys per tree per year.
- Nitrogen (Urea): 1–2 kg/tree split (after harvest and at flowering).
- Phosphorus (SSP/DAP) and Potash (SOP): 0.5–1 kg each per tree annually.
- Micronutrients (Zn, B, Fe) as foliar sprays if deficiency shows.

## Diseases
### Powdery Mildew
- White powdery coating on panicles/leaves; causes flower drop. Spray Sulphur/Wettable Sulphur or recommended fungicide per label at panicle emergence.

### Anthracnose (Black Tip)
- Black tips on fruits, spots on leaves. Manage with copper-oxide / recommended fungicide during flowering and pre-monsoon.

### Mango Malformation
- Abnormal clustered growth. Prune and destroy malformed shoots; use disease-free planting material.

## Pests
### Fruit Fly (*Bactrocera* spp.) — MAJOR
- Larvae tunnel ripening fruit. Use methyl eugenol traps, bait sprays, and harvest/collect fallen fruit; bag premium fruit.

### Hopper, Mealybug, Scale
- Cause sooty mould and drop. Manage with label insecticides / horticultural oil; conserve parasitoids.

## Common Problems
### Fruit Drop
- Physiological + water/heat stress. Steady irrigation and a light cover spray of micronutrients at fruit set.

### Small / Fibrous Fruit
- Overbearing; thin fruits and keep tree health.

### Sunburn / Skin Blemish
- Bag fruit or maintain canopy shade.

## Harvesting
- Harvest at correct maturity per variety (colour/shoulder change); handle with padded clippers; avoid bruising; pre-cool and grade.

## Local Measurements
- Yield varies widely; a healthy mature tree gives 100–200 kg fruit. Orchard spacing ~8–10 m.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Observe PHI before harvest; do not spray close to ripening fruit. Wear PPE; keep sprayers away from water channels and bees.
