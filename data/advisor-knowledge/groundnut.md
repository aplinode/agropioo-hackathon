# Groundnut (Peanut) — Complete Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Groundnut is an important oilseed and food crop in Pakistan, grown on approximately 0.15 million hectares. It is a Kharif season crop, primarily cultivated in Punjab (Cholistan, Thal) and Sindh (Tharparkar, Sanghar). Varieties include BARI-2000, BARI-2001, and local landraces.

## Crop Calendar

### Sowing
- **Optimal window:** May–June (after last expected frost, soil temperature >20°C)
- **Seed rate:** 80–100 kg per hectare (approximately 30–40 kg per acre)
- **Seed treatment:** Thiram + Rhizobium inoculant
- **Spacing:** Row-to-row 30 cm, plant-to-plant 15 cm

### Key Growth Stages
| Stage | Days After Sowing | Critical Management |
|---|---|---|
| Germination | 10–15 | Uniform moisture critical |
| Vegetative | 15–40 | First hoeing, weed management |
| Flowering | 40–55 | Pegging stage — loose soil needed |
| Pod filling | 55–80 | Critical water need |
| Maturity | 80–100 | Reduce irrigation, harvest timing |

### Irrigation Schedule
1. **First irrigation:** 20–25 days after sowing
2. **Second irrigation:** 40–45 days (flowering)
3. **Third irrigation:** 60–65 days (pod filling)
4. **Fourth irrigation:** 75–80 days (if needed)

Apply 40–50 mm per irrigation. Avoid waterlogging — groundnut is sensitive to excess moisture.

## Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
| Nutrient | Basal (at sowing) | Top-dress |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 10 kg (22 kg Urea) | — (fixes own N via Rhizobium) |
| Phosphorus | 25 kg (55 kg DAP) | — |
| Potassium | 15 kg (25 kg SOP) | — |

Total NPK: 10-25-15 kg per acre. Apply gypsum (calcium sulphate) at 200 kg/acre during pegging stage for better pod development.

## Major Diseases

### Collar Rot
- **Symptoms:** Browning and rotting at soil line, plant wilting
- **Favorable conditions:** Warm, wet soil
- **Management:** Seed treatment with Carboxin+Thiram, avoid waterlogging

### Cercospora Leaf Spot
- **Symptoms:** Small brown spots with yellow halos on leaves
- **Favorable conditions:** Warm, humid weather
- **Management:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5g/L water

### Rust
- **Symptoms:** Small brown pustules on leaves and pods
- **Management:** Spray Propiconazole 25% EC at 1ml/L water

## Major Pests

### Pod Borer (Helicoverpa)
- **Symptoms:** Larvae bore into pods, feed on developing kernels
- **Management:** Spray Emamectin benzoate 5% SG at 0.4g/L at flowering

### White Grub
- **Symptoms:** Root damage, plant wilting and death
- **Management:** Apply Chlorpyrifos 40% EC drenching at sowing

### Thrips
- **Symptoms:** Leaf curling, silvering, sticky honeydew
- **Management:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL at 0.3ml/L

## Harvesting and Storage
- Harvest when 70% of pods turn brown inside
- Uproot plants, stack upside down for 3–5 days to dry
- Thresh by beating or mechanical threshing
- Dry seeds to <8% moisture before storage
- Store in gunny bags in cool, dry place
- Expected yield: 15–25 maunds per acre (600–1000 kg/acre)

## Economic Considerations
- Seed cost: Rs 200–300 per kg
- Input cost per acre: Rs 20,000–30,000
- Expected revenue: Rs 45,000–75,000 per acre at Rs 6,000–8,000/maund
- Oil extraction rate: 40–45%
