# Onion — Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Onion is a Rabi bulb crop grown mainly through transplanting of seedlings. Major producing areas: Sindh (spring/summer crop), Punjab, and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (autumn/winter crop). It stores well and is a staple in Pakistani cooking.

### Recommended Varieties
- **Rehan-201, Nifa-2008, Phulkara, Desi (red), Golo** — pungent, good storage types.
- **Taher-2009, Saryab** — for drier zones.

## Crop Calendar
### Sowing / Nursery
- Nursery sowing: Sep–Nov (for winter crop). Transplant 6–8 week seedlings.
- Direct-seeded summer crop (Sindh) sown Dec–Feb.

### Key Growth Stages
- Seedling → vegetative leaf growth → bulb initiation → bulb enlargement → maturity (leaf fall / neck bending).

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Stages
- Bulb enlargement needs steady moisture; water stress causes small or split bulbs.
### Practical Schedule
- Keep nursery moist. After transplanting, irrigate every 7–10 days; reduce near maturity to harden bulbs. Withhold water 2–3 weeks before lifting.

## Fertilizer Management
### Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
- FYM: 6–8 trolleys at preparation.
- Nitrogen (Urea): 2–3 bags, split (avoid excess late N — delays maturity, reduces storage).
- Phosphorus (DAP): 2 bags at planting.
- Potash (SOP): 1–1.5 bags — improves bulb size and storage.

## Diseases
### Purple Blotch (*Alternaria porri*)
- Purple oval spots on leaves; reduces bulb size. Spray Mancozeb / Propineb per label at early signs.

### Downy Mildew
- Pale patches, grey fuzz in humid weather. Improve airflow; protectant fungicide.

### Basal Rot (*Fusarium oxysporum*)
- Bulbs rot in storage. Use healthy seed, avoid waterlogging, cure bulbs well before storage.

## Pests
### Thrips (*Thrips tabaci*) — MAJOR
- Silvery streaks, deformed leaves, poor bulb fill. Spray Spinosad / Imidacloprid per label; avoid herd build-up.

### Onion Fly / Cutworm
- Maggots cut seedlings; treat nursery/seedbed if damage is high.

## Common Problems
### Bolting (Flowering)
- Cold shock to seedlings causes premature flowering and no bulb. Use correct variety and timing; harden seedlings gradually.

### Double / Split Bulbs
- Uneven irrigation. Keep moisture steady during bulking.

### Poor Storage / Sprouting
- Harvest at correct maturity, cure in shade, store cool and dry, trim tops after cure.

## Harvesting
- Lift when tops fall and necks bend (130–160 days). Cure 7–10 days in shade, then trim and store in well-ventilated, dry conditions.

## Local Measurements
- Seedling rate: ~3–4 kg/acre nursery. Yield 150–300 maund/acre for good crops. 1 acre ≈ 8 kanal.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Spray in calm wind, wear PPE, observe PHI. Do not graze livestock on treated fields. Triple-rinse containers and dispose safely.
