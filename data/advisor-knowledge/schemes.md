# Government Agricultural Schemes in Pakistan (2024–2026)

This guide summarizes major federal and provincial government programs available to farmers in Pakistan. Details, eligibility criteria, and subsidy amounts change periodically. Always verify current information with your district agriculture office, the relevant implementing agency, or official government websites before applying.

---

## 1. Kissan Card Program

### What It Is

The Kissan Card is a government interest-free loan program launched to provide affordable credit to small and medium farmers for purchasing agricultural inputs (seed, fertilizer, pesticide, diesel) for Kharif and Rabi crops. The program is backed by federal and provincial budgets and distributed through partner banks.

### Benefit

Interest-free loans disbursed directly to a farmer's Kissan Card account. Funds can only be used for approved agricultural inputs at registered dealers. Loan amounts vary by landholding size and crop.

### Eligibility Criteria

- Must be a resident farmer of Pakistan with valid CNIC.
- Must own or lease agricultural land (land ownership or tenancy records required).
- Landholding typically capped at a maximum threshold (varies by province; commonly up to 12.5 acres for priority lending, with larger holdings eligible at different terms).
- Must not be a defaulter on previous government or bank loans.
- Must be registered in the provincial farmer database (e.g., Punjab's Kissan Registration System).

### Required Documents

- Valid CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card).
- Land ownership documents (Fard/Jamabandi) or registered lease agreement.
- Recent utility bill or proof of residence.
- Passport-size photographs.
- Bank account details (or the card itself serves as the account).

### How to Apply

1. Visit your nearest designated bank branch (National Bank of Pakistan, Bank of Punjab, Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited, and other participating institutions) or district agriculture office.
2. Submit the application form along with required documents.
3. Verification of land records and identity is conducted by the revenue department and agriculture department.
4. Upon approval, the Kissan Card is issued and the sanctioned amount is credited.
5. Loans are typically disbursed seasonally before sowing windows. Repayment is due after harvest.

### Implementing Agency

Federal Ministry of National Food Security & Research in coordination with provincial agriculture departments and partner banks. Provincial implementation varies — contact your district agriculture officer for local procedures.

### Important Notes

- Loan amounts, repayment periods, and eligible inputs are announced each season. Check with your district agriculture office for current season details.
- Misuse of Kissan Card funds (purchasing non-agricultural items) can result in card cancellation and legal action.
- The program has been scaled up significantly since 2024. Budget allocations and coverage expand periodically — check for updates.

## 2. Fertilizer Subsidy

### What It Is

The federal and provincial governments periodically subsidize key fertilizers (primarily DAP and Urea) to reduce input costs for farmers. Subsidy mechanisms vary: direct price reduction at dealer level, cash transfers to farmers, or manufacturer-level subsidies that lower retail prices.

### Benefit

Reduced purchase price for subsidized fertilizer bags. The exact subsidy amount per bag varies by season, fiscal year budget allocation, and international fertilizer prices.

### Eligibility Criteria

- Registered farmers holding valid CNIC.
- Landholding within the eligible range specified for the current subsidy cycle.
- Purchase through authorized dealers who participate in the subsidy scheme.
- Some subsidy cycles require registration in provincial farmer databases.

### Required Documents

- Valid CNIC.
- Proof of landholding (Fard/Jamabandi or lease document).
- Kissan Card (where applicable — some subsidy programs are linked to the Kissan Card system).

### How to Avail

1. Confirm the current subsidy status and eligible products with your district agriculture office or authorized fertilizer dealer.
2. Purchase subsidized fertilizer from registered dealers. Present your CNIC and/or Kissan Card at the point of sale.
3. Some subsidy programs operate through digital verification — the dealer enters your CNIC into the system and the subsidy is applied at the counter.
4. Retain your purchase receipt as proof.

### Implementing Agency

Ministry of National Food Security & Research (federal policy), provincial agriculture departments (implementation), and fertilizer manufacturers/dealers (distribution).

### Important Notes

- Subsidy rates fluctuate with government budgets and global fertilizer prices. Do not assume last season's rate applies this season.
- Counterfeit and substandard fertilizer is a widespread problem. Buy only from authorized dealers and check for holograms, batch numbers, and proper packaging.
- The government periodically announces subsidy adjustments through press releases and agriculture department notifications. Stay informed through your local agriculture extension office.

## 3. Solar Tube Well Subsidy

### What It Is

Government programs to help farmers convert diesel-powered or electric tube wells to solar energy. This reduces pumping costs and dependence on unreliable grid electricity and expensive diesel. Programs are offered at both federal and provincial levels with varying subsidy structures.

### Benefit

Financial subsidy covering a percentage of the solar tube well installation cost. The remaining amount is paid by the farmer, sometimes through interest-free installments.

### Eligibility Criteria

- Must own operational agricultural land with an existing tube well (diesel or electric).
- Valid CNIC and land ownership documents.
- Tube well must be registered with the relevant irrigation/power department.
- Landholding size requirements vary by program (some programs prioritize smallholders).
- No outstanding dues to electricity distribution companies (for electric tube well conversions).

### Required Documents

- Valid CNIC.
- Land ownership documents.
- Existing tube well registration or proof of operation.
- Electricity connection documents (for electric tube well conversion).
- Quotation from an approved solar installer/vendor.
- Bank account details.

### How to Apply

1. Contact your district agriculture office or the provincial energy/irrigation department to confirm the current program and eligibility.
2. Obtain a list of government-approved solar vendors/installers.
3. Get a quotation from an approved vendor.
4. Submit the application with required documents to the designated office.
5. Upon approval, the vendor installs the system. The subsidy is either deducted upfront or reimbursed after verification.
6. Post-installation inspection confirms compliance before final subsidy release.

### Implementing Agency

Provincial agriculture departments, provincial energy departments, and the Alternative Energy Development Board (AEDB) at the federal level. Specific programs may also involve the Pakistan Agricultural Development Fund.

### Important Notes

- Only use government-approved vendors and equipment specifications. Self-installed systems using non-approved components typically do not qualify for subsidies.
- Solar tube well capacity must match your bore depth and water requirement. An undersized system will not meet irrigation needs; an oversized system wastes subsidy.
- Maintenance responsibilities fall on the farmer. Budget for panel cleaning, inverter servicing, and battery replacement (if applicable).
- Net metering policies for agricultural solar connections vary by province. Ask about selling excess power back to the grid.

## 4. Seed Certification and Distribution

### What It Is

Provincial seed corporations and the Federal Seed Certification & Registration Department produce, certify, and distribute quality seed of approved varieties. Certified seed guarantees genetic purity, physical purity, germination standards, and freedom from seed-borne diseases.

### Benefit

Access to genetically pure, high-germination, disease-tested seed at regulated prices. Certified seed consistently outperforms farm-saved seed by 15–30% in yield trials.

### Eligibility Criteria

- Any registered farmer can purchase certified seed.
- Some subsidized seed distribution programs target smallholders or specific disaster-affected areas.
- Priority may be given to farmers registered in provincial databases during limited-supply periods.

### Required Documents

- Valid CNIC.
- For subsidized distributions: land ownership proof and registration in the farmer database.
- For regular purchases: no special documentation beyond payment.

### How to Obtain Certified Seed

1. Identify the recommended variety for your crop and region from the latest variety advisory released by the provincial agriculture department or crop research institute.
2. Visit the nearest Punjab Seed Corporation, Sindh Seed Corporation, KPK Seed Corporation, or authorized private seed dealer outlet.
3. Request the specific variety and quantity. Verify the seed tag/certificate on each bag — it should show lot number, germination percentage, purity, and certification authority stamp.
4. Retain the purchase receipt and seed tag. These are needed if you file a complaint about seed quality.

### Implementing Agencies

- **Federal:** Federal Seed Certification & Registration Department (FSC&RD) — sets standards and certifies private seed companies.
- **Punjab:** Punjab Seed Corporation (PSC).
- **Sindh:** Sindh Seed Corporation (SSC).
- **KPK:** KPK Seed Corporation.
- **Private sector:** Licensed private seed companies also produce and sell certified seed under FSC&RD oversight.

### Important Notes

- Always check the seed tag. Uncertified seed sold as "certified" is a common fraud. Genuine certified seed carries an official tag with certification number.
- Report suspected fake seed to the Seed Certification Department or Consumer Protection authorities. Keep evidence (bag, tag, receipt).
- Certified seed of newer, higher-yielding varieties is often in limited supply. Order early before the sowing season.
- Hybrid seed (maize, vegetables) must be purchased fresh each season. Saving hybrid seed produces unpredictable, inferior offspring.

## 5. Crop Insurance Programs

### What It Is

The Government of Pakistan, in collaboration with provincial governments and insurance companies, has introduced crop insurance programs to protect farmers against losses from natural disasters (floods, drought, hailstorm, pest outbreaks). Coverage scope and availability have expanded since the devastating 2022 floods highlighted the need for agricultural risk management.

### Benefit

Financial compensation for verified crop losses due to covered perils. Payouts help farmers recover input costs and maintain livelihoods after disaster.

### Eligibility Criteria

- Registered farmers with documented landholdings.
- Enrollment must occur before the sowing season or within the designated enrollment window.
- Coverage typically applies to major crops (wheat, rice, maize, cotton, sugarcane) — specialty crops may or may not be included depending on the program.
- Farmers must follow recommended agronomic practices. Negligence or deviation from standard practices may void claims.

### Required Documents

- Valid CNIC.
- Land ownership/tenancy documents.
- Crop sowing declaration (area, variety, date).
- Bank account details for claim disbursement.
- In case of claim: photographic evidence, agriculture department damage assessment report.

### How to Apply

1. Inquire about available crop insurance programs at your district agriculture office. Programs may be administered through partner insurance companies, microfinance institutions, or provincial disaster management authorities.
2. Enroll during the designated window (typically before or at the start of the sowing season). Pay any required premium (some programs are fully subsidized; others require a nominal farmer contribution).
3. In case of crop damage, report to the agriculture department within the stipulated timeframe (usually 7–15 days of the event).
4. A damage assessment team visits the field and verifies losses.
5. Approved claims are paid to the farmer's bank account.

### Implementing Agencies

Provincial Disaster Management Authorities (PDMA), State Bank of Pakistan (regulatory framework for index-based insurance), partner insurance companies, and provincial agriculture departments.

### Important Notes

- Crop insurance programs in Pakistan are still evolving. Availability, coverage terms, and claim processes vary significantly by province and year. Do not assume a program available last year is unchanged.
- Index-based insurance (payout triggered by weather station data rather than individual field assessment) is being piloted in some areas. Understand whether your policy is indemnity-based or index-based.
- Document everything: photograph your crop at regular intervals, keep input purchase receipts, and maintain records of all farming operations. Documentation strengthens claims.
- Insurance does not replace good farm management. It is a safety net, not a substitute for proper irrigation, pest control, and agronomy.

## 6. Punjab Agriculture Department Subsidies

The Punjab Agriculture Department runs multiple subsidy and support programs beyond those listed above. Key programs include:

### Laser Land Leveling Subsidy

- **Benefit:** Subsidized laser land leveling service to achieve precise field grading, improving irrigation efficiency by 20–30% and reducing water wastage.
- **Eligibility:** Landowning farmers in Punjab with uneven fields. Priority often given to small and medium landholders.
- **How to apply:** Contact your district agriculture office or the On-Farm Water Management (OFWM) section. Submit land documents and request leveling service. Government teams or approved contractors perform the work.
- **Implementing agency:** Punjab Agriculture Department, On-Farm Water Management Directorate.

### Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation Subsidy

- **Benefit:** Financial subsidy for installing drip or sprinkler irrigation systems. Covers a portion of equipment and installation costs. Promotes water conservation, particularly for orchards, vegetables, and high-value crops.
- **Eligibility:** Farmers in Punjab with suitable crops and water source. Landholding and crop-type criteria apply.
- **How to apply:** Apply through the district agriculture office or the Punjab Irrigation Department's High-Efficiency Irrigation Systems (HEIS) program. Submit land documents, cropping pattern details, and water source information. Approved installations are inspected before subsidy release.
- **Implementing agency:** Punjab Agriculture Department and Punjab Irrigation Department.

### Other Punjab Programs (Subject to Change)

- **Farm mechanization subsidies:** Tractors, harvesters, and other machinery at subsidized rates or through installment plans.
- **Soil testing:** Free or subsidized soil testing at government laboratories. Essential for rational fertilizer use.
- **Training and extension:** Free farmer training programs on modern agronomic practices, pest management, and post-harvest handling through agriculture extension offices and Farmer Field Schools.
- **Livestock support programs:** Vaccination campaigns, breed improvement, and fodder seed distribution.

### How to Stay Informed About Punjab Programs

- Visit your district agriculture office regularly. Officers maintain updated lists of active programs.
- Follow the Punjab Agriculture Department's official website and social media channels.
- Attend farmer meetings and field days organized by extension services.
- Contact the Punjab Agriculture Helpline if available in your district.

## General Guidance for All Schemes

### Before Applying

1. **Verify current details.** Program terms change frequently. Always confirm eligibility, documents, and deadlines with the implementing agency before applying.
2. **Register early.** Many programs require prior registration in provincial farmer databases. Complete registration well before the application window opens.
3. **Keep documents ready.** Maintain updated copies of CNIC, land records, bank account details, and recent utility bills. Having these ready speeds up every application.
4. **Use official channels only.** Deal only with government offices, authorized banks, and approved vendors. Middlemen and unofficial agents frequently charge illegal commissions and submit fraudulent applications.

### Fraud Awareness

- Government officers do NOT charge fees for processing applications. Any demand for payment is likely corrupt. Report to anti-corruption authorities.
- Never share your CNIC number, bank details, or Kissan Card PIN with anyone claiming to "help" you apply.
- Verify SMS messages claiming to be from government programs by calling the official helpline. Phishing scams targeting farmers are increasing.
- If something seems too good to be true, verify it with your district agriculture office before acting.

### Where to Get Help

- **District Agriculture Office:** First point of contact for all provincial programs.
- **Tehsil Agriculture Extension Officer:** Closer to your village; handles field-level queries.
- **Bank branches:** For Kissan Card and loan-related queries.
- **Provincial Agriculture Department helplines:** Phone numbers vary by province; ask at your district office.
- **Pakistan Agricultural Information Service:** Provides crop advisories and scheme information.
