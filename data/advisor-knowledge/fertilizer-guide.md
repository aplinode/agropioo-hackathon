# Practical Guide: Fertilizer Application in Pakistan

## Overview
This guide provides practical, step-by-step instructions for fertilizer application for major Pakistani crops. Always use soil test results when available. When soil test is not available, follow general recommendations for your crop and region.

## General Principles

### When to Apply
- **Basal fertilizer (DAP, SOP):** At sowing time, mixed with seed drill or broadcast before last plowing
- **Top-dress nitrogen (Urea):** At the recommended growth stage, not before
- **Avoid fertilizing:** During strong winds (>15 km/h), during rain, or when soil is waterlogged

### How to Apply
- **Urea:** Broadcast on moist soil, then irrigate within 24 hours or apply before expected rain
- **DAP:** Place 5 cm below seed level in furrow, not directly touching seed
- **SOP:** Broadcast and mix with soil, or apply through irrigation water

### Safety Precautions
- Wear gloves when handling fertilizers
- Wash hands thoroughly after application
- Store fertilizers in dry, covered area away from food and drinking water
- Keep away from children and livestock

## Crop-Specific Recommendations

### Wheat (Rabi season)
| Nutrient | Basal (at sowing) | Top-dress (at CRI) |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 30 kg/acre (65 kg Urea) | 30 kg/acre (65 kg Urea) |
| Phosphorus | 35 kg/acre (76 kg DAP) | — |
| Potassium | 15 kg/acre (25 kg SOP) | — |

**Timing:** Basal at sowing, top-dress at first irrigation (CRI stage, 20–25 days after sowing)
**Tip:** Split nitrogen into 2 doses — basal + CRI — for best results

### Cotton (Kharif season)
| Nutrient | Basal (at sowing) | Top-dress (at square formation) |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 30 kg/acre (65 kg Urea) | 30 kg/acre (65 kg Urea) |
| Phosphorus | 25 kg/acre (55 kg DAP) | — |
| Potassium | 15 kg/acre (25 kg SOP) | — |

**Timing:** Basal at sowing, top-dress at square formation (45–50 days after sowing)
**Tip:** Cotton responds well to foliar application of micronutrients (Zinc, Boron)

### Rice (Kharif season)
| Nutrient | Basal (at transplanting) | Top-dress 1 | Top-dress 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 25 kg/acre (55 kg Urea) | 25 kg/acre (55 kg Urea) at tillering | 25 kg/acre (55 kg Urea) at panicle initiation |
| Phosphorus | 25 kg/acre (55 kg DAP) | — | — |
| Potassium | 15 kg/acre (25 kg SOP) | — | — |

**Timing:** Basal at transplanting, top-dress at tillering (25–30 days) and panicle initiation (55–60 days)
**Tip:** Apply Urea to standing water in paddy — do not drain before application

### Sugarcane (Annual crop)
| Nutrient | Basal (at planting) | Top-dress 1 | Top-dress 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nitrogen | 40 kg/acre (87 kg Urea) | 40 kg/acre (87 kg Urea) at 60 days | 40 kg/acre (87 kg Urea) at 120 days |
| Phosphorus | 30 kg/acre (65 kg DAP) | — | — |
| Potassium | 20 kg/acre (33 kg SOP) | — | — |

**Timing:** Basal at planting, top-dress at 60 and 120 days
**Tip:** Sugarcane is a heavy feeder — do not skip nitrogen applications

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Over-application of Urea:** Causes lodging, delayed maturity, and disease susceptibility
2. **Applying to dry soil:** Fertilizer won't reach roots — always apply to moist soil or irrigate after
3. **Ignoring phosphorus:** DAP is critical for root development — don't skip it
4. **Wrong timing:** Top-dressing nitrogen too late wastes fertilizer and reduces yield
5. **Uneven application:** Use broadcast or seed drill for uniform distribution

## Micronutrients
### Zinc Sulphate
- **When:** Basal application
- **Rate:** 10–15 kg/acre
- **Crops:** Rice, wheat, cotton
- **Signs of deficiency:** Stunted growth, small leaves, white rust in rice

### Borax
- **When:** At sowing or foliar spray at flowering
- **Rate:** 5–10 kg/acre (soil) or 2g/L water (foliar)
- **Crops:** Cotton, sunflower, mustard
- **Signs of deficiency:** Poor flowering, hollow stem, cracked pods
