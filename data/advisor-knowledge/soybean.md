# Soybean — Crop Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Soybean is a Kharif oilseed and protein crop gaining area in Punjab and Sindh, often after wheat or as a cotton replacement. It fixes nitrogen and produces edible oil and high-protein meal. Best on well-drained, fertile loam.

### Recommended Varieties
- **Naveed-2023, Ajmer, Williams-82, NARC-3, Sahil-2007** — adapted, moderate-maturity types.

## Crop Calendar
### Sowing
- Sow mid-June to mid-July (after wheat harvest / cotton gaps). Late sowing drops yield.
- Row spacing 30–45 cm; seed treated with Rhizobium + PSB.

### Key Growth Stages
- Germination → vegetative → flowering (40–55 days) → pod fill → maturity (100–120 days).

## Irrigation Schedule
### Critical Stages
- Flowering and pod filling need moisture; one irrigation at flowering and one at pod fill on rainfed/light soils is highly profitable.
### Practical Schedule
- Typically rainfed in monsoon; supplement if dry spells at flowering. Avoid waterlogging.

## Fertilizer Management
### Fertilizer Schedule (per acre)
- Usually minimal N (fixes own). If poor soil, 1 bag Urea at sowing.
- Phosphorus (DAP): 1–1.5 bags at sowing.
- Potash (SOP): 0.5–1 bag on sandy soils.
- Rhizobium + PSB seed treatment essential for nodulation and yield.

## Diseases
### Charcoal Rot (*Macrophomina phaseolina*)
- Causes sudden wilting and stem charcoal-like specks in hot, dry conditions. Use resistant varieties, avoid moisture stress.

### Frog-Eye Leaf Spot (*Cercospora*)
- Small grey spots with red borders. Spray Mancozeb / Carbendazim per label if severe.

### Root Rot
- On poorly drained soils. Ensure drainage and crop rotation.

### Yellow Mosaic Virus
- Spread by whitefly/beetles; use healthy seed and control vectors.

## Pests
### Stem Fly / Leaf Folder
- Early-season seedling damage. Treat seed or seedlings if threshold exceeded.

### Whitefly, Hairy Caterpillar, Defoliators
- Scout; use Bt or label insecticides only above threshold to protect natural enemies.

## Common Problems
### Poor Nodulation
- Seed not inoculated. Always treat with Rhizobium + PSB.

### Lodging
- On rich soil with excess growth; avoid extra nitrogen and dense sowing.

### Shrivelled Seed
- Drought at pod fill. Irrigate at pod fill if possible.

## Harvesting
- Harvest when leaves yellow and pods are tan/brown (moisture <14%). Cut, dry, thresh; store dry to prevent aflatoxin.

## Local Measurements
- Seed rate ≈ 1.5–2 maund/acre (inoculated). Yield 15–30 maund/acre (good seasons). 1 acre ≈ 8 kanal.

## Chemical Safety Warnings
- Wear PPE when spraying; observe PHI. Store grain dry and cool; discard mouldy seed — do not feed to livestock.
