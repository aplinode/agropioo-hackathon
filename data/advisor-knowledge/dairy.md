# Dairy Cattle and Buffalo — Guide for Pakistan

## Overview
Dairy (cattle and buffalo) is the backbone of Pakistan's milk supply. Key breeds: **Sahiwal, Cholistani** (cattle); **Nili-Ravi, Kundi, Azi-Kheli** (buffalo). Management focuses on feeding, breeding, comfort, and disease prevention for profitable milk.

### Breeds / Types
- **Cattle:** Sahiwal (heat-tolerant, good milk), Cholistani, crossbred (Holstein/Friesian × local for higher yield).
- **Buffalo:** Nili-Ravi (high fat), Kundi.

## Housing
- Shade, ventilation, and clean dry bedding; ridges/curbs for drainage.
- Tie or loose housing; protect from heat (fan/misting in summer) — heat cuts milk and fertility.

## Feeding
### Principles
- Quality fodder (berseem, maize silage, oat), concentrates (cottonseed cake, oilcake, grain), and clean water (40–60 L/animal/day for milch animals).
- Balance energy, protein, minerals (salt + DCP), and vitamins.
- Feed after milking; avoid sudden ration changes (causes acidosis/tympany).

### Mineral / Urea Blocks
- Salt + mineral blocks free-choice; urea-molasses blocks only for ruminants and never exceed safe levels.

## Breeding / Reproduction
- Heat detection (standing to mount), AI from trained technicians; calving interval target ~12–13 months.
- Dry period 45–60 days before calving for udder rest.

## Diseases
### Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD)
- Salivation, lameness, blisters. **Notifiable** — vaccinate routinely; isolate and report outbreaks; veterinarians lead control.

### Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS)
- Sudden fever, respiratory distress, death; vaccinate in risk seasons (monsoon/winter); urgent vet care.

### Mastitis
- Swollen, hot udder, abnormal milk. Practice teat dipping, dry-cow therapy, clean milking; treat under vet direction. **Discard milk during treatment per withdrawal.**

### Milk Fever (Hypocalcaemia)
- Downer cow around calving; immediate vet calcium treatment.

### Bloat / Tympany
- From lush legume/rapid feed change; relieve with antifermentives, walking, vet if severe.

### Worms / Ticks / Lice
- Parasite control program; clean pastures; acaricides per label.

## Common Problems
### Low Milk Yield
- Review feeding, water, heat, and health; milk record per animal.

### Repeat Breeding / Anoestrus
- Body condition, nutrition, and silent heat; use trained AI and veterinary check.

### Calf Mortality
- Colostrum within 2–4 hours of birth, clean calving, vaccination.

## Marketing
- Sell milk chilled; keep records; cull non-productive animals.

## Safety & Handling Warnings
- Handle quietly; use chutes. **Report notifiable diseases (FMD, HS) to a vet / department immediately.**
- Withhold milk/meat per withdrawal after any medicine.
- Store vaccines cold (cold chain); dispose of needles safely.
- Keep feed storage rodent-proof; avoid mouldy feed (mycotoxins).
